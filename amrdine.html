Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the POS really print orders when the internet is completely disconnected?

Yes, 100%! All cashier registers, kitchen printers, and bar displays communicate directly through your local Wi-Fi router (LAN) without touching the public internet. Transactions continue at full speed.

What hardware equipment do I need to run MRD Solution?

You can use standard affordable hardware: Windows PC or Mini PC for the local server, Android tablets or iPads for cashiers/waiters, and standard 58mm/80mm ESC/POS thermal printers (Ethernet/LAN/USB).

How does the City Ledger AR feature benefit our restaurant accounting?

It prevents the classic nightmare of double-counting. When guests or owners eat on credit today and pay next week, the system correctly logs food revenue on the consumption date and logs cash intake on the payment date.
